Name: DaddyCare Quiz
Subtitle: Learn parenting by stages · 5–10 questions/day

Description:
DaddyCare Quiz is a “learn-by-quizzing” app for parents. It provides curated question banks across key topics (nutrition, sleep, motor skills, cognition, social) aligned with a child’s development stages (0–6 months, 6–12 months, 1–3 years). Practice 5–10 questions a day with instant feedback and practical tips to build a solid understanding of parenting step by step.

Features:
- Staged learning by age;
- Single/Multi choice questions;
- Instant result with explanations;
- Wrong-question notebook for review;
- Progress & pie-chart statistics;
- On-device storage only (no account required);
- Not medical advice — for educational purposes only.
