# Privacy Policy (DaddyCare Quiz)

Updated: 2025-11-16  
Contact: sealday@gmail.com

## What We Collect
This app is an offline learning tool and does not collect personally identifiable information. We do not track ads and do not share data with third parties. Data stored locally on your device includes:
- Quiz progress and stage completion
- Wrong questions and viewing history
- Daily quiz counts

All the above data is kept on-device and never uploaded to a server.

## How We Use Data
- To display progress, stats, and review features within the app only.
- Not used for ads or cross-app tracking.

## Control & Deletion
- Use “Clear Local Data” in the app’s Settings/About page to delete local data at any time.
- Reinstalling the app clears all local data.

## Third-Party Services
This version does not include analytics, ads, or login SDKs. If added in the future, we will update this policy and notify users within the app.

## Children & Sensitive Info
This app is intended for adults (parents/guardians). Minors should not use it unsupervised. Please do not submit sensitive personal information in feedback.

## Policy Updates
If we make significant changes, we will update this page and highlight it in the app’s Settings/About page. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
